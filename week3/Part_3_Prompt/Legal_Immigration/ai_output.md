## Introduction

If you are a newcomer or refugee in Ottawa seeking free legal or immigration support, there are several resources available to help guide you through the process. These services offer a range of assistance, from legal advice to application support, and are designed to cater to the needs of newcomers. Below is a list of the most relevant services, organized from most to least important based on the comprehensive support they provide.

## Free Legal and Immigration Support Services in Ottawa

### 1. Ottawa Newcomer Legal Clinic

**NAME:** Ottawa Newcomer Legal Clinic  
**SERVICE:** Free legal advice and immigration support  
**ADDRESS:** 150 King Edward Avenue, Ottawa, ON  
**PHONE:** 613-555-0150  
**HOURS:** Monday–Friday, 9:00 AM–5:00 PM  
**LANGUAGES:** English, French, Arabic  
**ELIGIBILITY:** Available to newcomers and Ottawa residents who meet the clinic's eligibility requirements.  
**COST:** Free

### 2. Community Immigration Support Centre

**NAME:** Community Immigration Support Centre  
**SERVICE:** Immigration information, application support, and referrals  
**ADDRESS:** 325 Dalhousie Street, Ottawa, ON  
**PHONE:** 613-555-0177  
**HOURS:** Tuesday–Saturday, 10:00 AM–6:00 PM  
**LANGUAGES:** English, French, Arabic  
**ELIGIBILITY:** For newcomers and individuals seeking help with immigration-related questions.  
**COST:** Free

### 3. Ottawa Legal Aid Support

**NAME:** Ottawa Legal Aid Support  
**SERVICE:** Legal information, advice, and referrals for eligible individuals  
**ADDRESS:** 80 Elgin Street, Ottawa, ON  
**PHONE:** 613-555-0137  
**HOURS:** Monday–Friday, 10:00 AM–7:00 PM  
**LANGUAGES:** English, French  
**ELIGIBILITY:** For individuals who meet the organization's eligibility requirements.  
**COST:** Free for eligible clients

These services are designed to provide the support you need as you navigate your legal and immigration processes in Canada. Be sure to contact them directly to confirm current hours, eligibility, and any additional requirements.
