## Introduction

Welcome! If you're seeking free mental health support in Ottawa, there are several resources available tailored to meet your needs. These services offer counselling and support groups to help with stress, isolation, adjustment difficulties, and more. All services listed are free, but some may have specific eligibility requirements and may require an intake appointment. The services are available in multiple languages to ensure accessibility. Below is a list of these resources organised from most to least relevant based on your needs as a newcomer or refugee.

## Free Mental Health Support Services in Ottawa

### 1. Newcomer Wellness Program
**NAME:** Newcomer Wellness Program  
**SERVICE:** Mental health counselling and newcomer support groups  
**ADDRESS:** 325 Dalhousie Street, Ottawa, ON  
**PHONE:** 613-555-0177  
**HOURS:** Tuesday–Saturday, 10:00 AM–6:00 PM  
**LANGUAGES:** English, French, Arabic  
**ELIGIBILITY:** For newcomers experiencing stress, isolation, or adjustment difficulties.  
**COST:** Free  

### 2. Ottawa Community Wellness Centre
**NAME:** Ottawa Community Wellness Centre  
**SERVICE:** Counselling and mental health support  
**ADDRESS:** 150 King Edward Avenue, Ottawa, ON  
**PHONE:** 613-555-0150  
**HOURS:** Monday–Friday, 9:00 AM–5:00 PM  
**LANGUAGES:** English, French, Arabic  
**ELIGIBILITY:** Services are available to Ottawa residents. Intake may be required.  
**COST:** Free  

### 3. Youth Counselling Services
**NAME:** Youth Counselling Services  
**SERVICE:** Short-term counselling for youth  
**ADDRESS:** 80 Elgin Street, Ottawa, ON  
**PHONE:** 613-555-0137  
**HOURS:** Monday–Friday, 10:00 AM–7:00 PM  
**LANGUAGES:** English, French  
**ELIGIBILITY:** For youth ages 13–24.  
**COST:** Free
